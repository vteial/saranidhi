## Release notes (body)

The "Aruḍam Now" verdict now **explains itself**. A new **"Why?"** section lets you see the reasoning behind the answer — in plain language, with the traditional source each factor rests on.

### 💡 What's New
- **"Why?" accordion on the Aruḍam Now card** — tap to expand a plain-language explanation of the verdict. It's collapsed by default, so the card stays glanceable.
- **Grouped the way you think about it** — reasons appear under **Moment — the timing** (your bird state, the planetary hour, your birth-star strength, and how well your activity fits the window) and **You — your readiness** (whether your breath is naturally aligned). During an inauspicious window, a single **Blocked** explanation is shown instead.
- **Every reason cites its source** — each line carries a small reference to the traditional practice / confirmation it comes from, so the guidance is transparent and traceable. Saranidhi never shows raw arithmetic.
- Fully bilingual (English + தமிழ்).

### Notes
- **Transparency only** — this release doesn't change *what* the verdict says, only how clearly it explains *why*. Scoring, bands, the inauspicious floor-lock, and the Prasanam Oracle are all unchanged.
