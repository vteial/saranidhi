## Release notes (body)

Saranidhi now notices when your breath rhythm has **drifted** — and gently suggests how to rebalance. Present-moment, self-observable guidance; never a diagnosis, never a prediction.

### 🌡️ What's New
- **Breath-stagnancy awareness** — if your breath has stayed on one channel too long over the past day, a gentle card appears on Home (**mild** around 6 hours, **longer** around 8). It stays hidden when your rhythm is healthy, and a Sushumna (central) reading resets the count.
- **Warming vs cooling correction** — stuck on the **right** (solar) channel leans hot, so the card suggests **cooling** (Sheetali breath, cool fluids, calm); stuck on the **left** (lunar) channel leans cold/sluggish, so it suggests **warming** (Surya Bhedana breath, warming spices, gentle movement). A one-tap option opens the breath-reset to help you shift.
- **Eating by the breath (Swara-Ahara)** — during an action/nourishment window, the Focus Card tells you whether your digestive fire is well-placed: right nostril active → good time to eat; left nostril active → a gentle nudge to reset first.
- **Waking rule (Swara Pada Gamana)** — your optional morning summary now reminds you to step down first with the foot on your active-nostril side.

### 🌿 The spirit of it
- **A nudge, not a rule.** Every suggestion points toward *natural* alignment cultivated through consistency — never a mandate, never a medical claim.
