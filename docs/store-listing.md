## Full Description (Both Stores)

### English

Saranidhi — The Treasure House of Breath

Align your daily breath patterns with cosmic rhythms using the ancient sciences of Sara Kalai (Siva Swarodaya) and Panja Pakshi Shastra.

KEY FEATURES:

- Breath Alignment Tracking — Log your active nostril (Solar/Lunar/Sushumna) and instantly see if you're aligned with the cosmic pattern
- Panja Pakshi Engine — Authentic bird state calculations using traditional 2D lookup tables for all weekdays and lunar phases
- Vedic Time System — Sunrise/sunset, 5 Yamas, Rahu Kaal, Hora, and Tattva cycles calculated offline
- Streak & Consistency — Track your alignment streak, 7-day ribbon, 30-day trend, and Yama coverage
- AI Wisdom — Daily spiritual insights from a curated wisdom library rooted in traditional teachings
- Breath Timer — Guided inhale/hold/exhale with live seconds display
- Quick Sync Pacer — Animated breathing guide to help shift your dominant nostril
- Complete Privacy — All data stays on your device. Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero server storage

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE:
All calculations use pure mathematical algorithms based on your location and the current date. No internet connection required.

BILINGUAL:
Full support for English and Tamil (தமிழ்) with localized spiritual terminology.

BEAUTIFUL THEMES:
8 Material 3 theme variants — 4 colors (Default, Emerald, Gold, Purple) in Light and Dark modes, plus System auto-switching.

Saranidhi is built on the principle that conscious breath awareness is the foundation of spiritual growth. Whether you practice Pranayama, Swara Yoga, or simply want to live more consciously, this app guides you toward alignment with the natural cosmic rhythms that govern all life.
