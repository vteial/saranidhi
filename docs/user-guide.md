## What is Saranidhi?

**Saranidhi** (சரநிதி) means **"The Treasure House of Breath"** — a profound combination of two ancient Sanskrit/Tamil words:

- **Sara (சர)** — Refers to *Swara* or *Charam*, the sacred flow of breath through the nostrils. In *Siva Swarodaya* and traditional Tamil *Sara Kalai* sciences, Sara is the dynamic life-force energy (Prana) that shifts between the Solar channel (Pingala — right nostril) and Lunar channel (Ida — left nostril).

- **Nidhi (நிதி)** — Means *Treasure*, *Repository*, or *Wealth*. It represents an inexhaustible storehouse of divine value.

Saranidhi is a spiritual life-guidance app that helps you treat your daily breath patterns as a form of divine wealth. By observing and aligning your internal breath flow with external cosmic currents, you unlock a state of optimal living, presence, and vitality.

## The Aim

Saranidhi exists to bridge the ancient wisdom of breath science with modern daily life. Its aim is threefold:

### 1. Awareness
Help you notice the subtle but powerful rhythm of your breath — which nostril is dominant right now, how long your breath flows, and what that means for your current state of being.

### 2. Alignment
Guide you to synchronize your personal breath rhythm with the cosmic patterns described in Siva Swarodaya — the hours of planetary power (Horas), the five elemental cycles (Tattvas), and the biological rhythms of the five sacred birds (Panja Pakshi).

### 3. Transformation
Through consistent daily observation and alignment, cultivate a conscious way of living where every breath becomes an act of spiritual practice — not something you do, but something you *are*.

## Core Concepts

### The Three Flows

Your breath flows through one of three states at any given moment:

| Flow | Nostril | Energy | Nature |
|------|---------|--------|--------|
| **Solar (Surya Swara)** | Right (Pingala) | Active, warming, masculine | Best for physical tasks, courage, difficult conversations |
| **Lunar (Chandra Swara)** | Left (Ida) | Receptive, cooling, feminine | Best for creative work, learning, peaceful activities |
| **Sushumna** | Both equally | Balanced, transcendent | Rare and sacred — ideal for meditation and spiritual practice |

### The Five Yamas (Time Segments)

Each day, from sunrise to sunset, is divided into **5 equal time segments** called Yamas. Each Yama activates a different quality of energy, mapped through the Panja Pakshi system.

### The Five Sacred Birds (Panja Pakshi)

Based on your birth star (Nakshatra) and the current lunar phase, one of five birds is assigned as your guiding bird:

| Bird | Quality | When Ruling |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Vulture (Hawk)** | Power, authority | Take bold decisions |
| **Owl** | Wisdom, insight | Study, plan, strategize |
| **Crow** | Movement, activity | Travel, communicate, network |
| **Rooster (Cock)** | Nourishment, growth | Eat, heal, build |
| **Peacock** | Grace, display | Present, perform, celebrate |

Each bird cycles through five states daily: **Ruling → Eating → Walking → Sleeping → Dying**. When your bird is *Ruling*, you are at peak power. When *Dying*, conserve energy.

### Rahu Kaal (The Shadow Period)

Each day contains one inauspicious window called **Rahu Kaal** — a period governed by the shadow planet Rahu. During this time, Saranidhi signals caution: the Oracle Readiness drops to 10%, advising you to avoid starting new ventures.

### Hora (Planetary Hours)

Each hour of the day is governed by a planet in the Chaldean sequence. Knowing the active Hora helps you choose activities aligned with planetary strengths (e.g., Jupiter's hora for learning, Venus's hora for relationships).

### Tattva (Elemental Breath)

Within each breath cycle, the five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether) rotate in sequence. Advanced practitioners can sense which element is active by observing breath characteristics — length, warmth, and direction of airflow.

## Features at a Glance

### 🫁 Breath Journal (Sara Kalai)
Log your current breath flow with just two taps. See instantly whether you're aligned with the cosmic pattern. Optionally time your breath to track inhale, hold, and exhale durations. A **guided nostril test** walks you through checking your dominant flow — Lunar (left), Sushumna (both), or Solar (right) — with a **Start over** button to reset at any point.

### 🌬️ Clear Breath Channel (Somatic Intervention)
When you log a breath and it's **not aligned** with the cosmic expectation, a **Clear Breath Channel** action appears. It offers two gentle, time-bound protocols to shift your dominant nostril toward the aligned state:

- **Posture Shift (3 min)** — lie on one side (lateral recumbency). Because breathing channels respond to *contralateral* pressure, lying on one side opens the *opposite* nostril.
- **Axillary Pressure (5 min)** — apply firm pressure under the opposite armpit (the classic Yoga Danda technique).

A full-screen room guides you with the correct body position, a **Sama Vritti** breathing pacer (equal-ratio 4:4:4:4 — inhale, hold, exhale, hold), and a high-contrast countdown. When the timer ends, the app re-runs the guided nostril test to verify whether your flow shifted, and records the session so you can review what works for you over time.

### 🔥 Streak & Consistency
Watch your alignment streak grow day by day. A 7-day visual ribbon shows your recent history. A 30-day trend reveals your deeper patterns. The longer your streak, the more attuned you become.

### 🦚 Panja Pakshi Oracle
Know your birth bird and its current state at any moment. See at a glance whether this is a time to act boldly (Ruling) or rest quietly (Sleeping). The Oracle Readiness score synthesizes all cosmic factors into a single actionable number.

### 🔮 Prasanam Oracle
A sacred consultation tool for life decisions. Select your query category (Artha for material, Kriya for action, Yoga for spiritual), center your intention, and receive a multi-factor composite score:

- **Score Gauge** (0–100) — synthesizes bird state, hora-swara alignment, tarabala, and category harmony
- **5 Answer Bands** — Strong Yes (Siddha), Favorable (Vardhana), Caution (Mandha), Delay (Stambhana), Hard No (Sunya)
- **Validation Gate** — requires fresh breath awareness (within 30 minutes) before consultation
- **Window Status** — shows if current timing is favorable or void-locked (Rahu Kaal / Emakandam)
- **History** — optionally save readings for post-event reflection and outcome tracking. Delete an entry by swiping it on touch devices, or by hovering over it and clicking the trash icon on desktop

### ⏰ Smart Reminders
Receive gentle nudges at each Yama transition — the exact moment when cosmic energy shifts. Each notification carries a piece of traditional wisdom tailored to your current bird state.

### 🧠 AI Wisdom
A daily insight card powered by on-device intelligence. Your streak, accuracy, active bird, and current cosmic conditions are woven together into a personalized spiritual coaching note.

### 🌓 Cosmic Dashboard
A single-glance view showing: active Hora (planetary hour), current Tattva (element), Rahu Kaal status, and your bird's state. Everything you need to navigate the day consciously.

### 🔒 Complete Privacy
Your breath data is sacred and personal. It never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up — and even then, it goes only to your own iCloud or Google Drive account. We never see, store, or process your data.

### 🌙 Beautiful Themes
Four handcrafted visual themes to match your mood and practice: Light (clarity), Dark (night practice), Emerald (growth), and Gold (abundance).

### 🌐 Bilingual
Full support for English and Tamil (தமிழ்), honoring the Tamil origins of Sara Kalai science.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Open Saranidhi
On first launch, a gentle onboarding guides you through setup.

### Step 2: Find Your Birth Bird
Choose one of three ways to determine your birth bird — presented as three co-equal tabs:

- **I know my star** — Pick your Nakshatra (lunar mansion) directly.
- **Calculate from DOB** *(most accurate)* — Enter your date of birth, time, and birth place. Saranidhi computes your exact Nakshatra and, using your birth lunar phase (Paksha), derives your permanent birth bird from the correct Bright/Dark half table.
- **Calculate from name** — A quick fallback that maps the first vowel sound of your name to a bird when you don't know your star or DOB.

> **Tip:** The DOB path is the most reliable, because the birth bird depends on both your Nakshatra *and* whether the moon was waxing or waning at birth.

### Step 3: Allow Location
Saranidhi uses your **current** location to calculate precise sunrise and sunset times for your area, keeping all Yama, Hora, and Rahu calculations accurate to your exact position. On the web, if you travel more than 5 km from your saved location, the app quietly updates it on next open and shows a brief notice (your birth bird never changes — only the daily rhythm follows your position). You can always edit the location manually in Settings.

### Step 4: Choose Your Storage
- **Local Only** — Data stays on your device. Maximum privacy. (Mobile only)
- **Cloud Backup** — Data backs up to your iCloud (iOS) or Google Drive (Android/Web). Your data, your account.

### Step 5: Review & Confirm
A summary page shows everything you entered — birth bird, location, and storage choice. Each row has an **Edit** button so you can jump back and adjust any answer before tapping **Complete Setup**.

### Step 6: Breathe
Close one nostril. Notice which side flows more freely. Tap to log. That's your first entry in the Treasure House of Breath.

## The Science Behind Saranidhi

Saranidhi is built on three ancient Indian scientific systems:

### 1. Siva Swarodaya (शिव स्वरोदय)
A text of 395 sutras in which Lord Shiva reveals to Parvati the science of breath. It describes how the alternation of breath between nostrils governs health, fortune, and spiritual progress. The text provides specific guidelines for which activities to perform based on the active nostril.

### 2. Panja Pakshi Shastra (பஞ்ச பக்ஷி சாஸ்திரம்)
An ancient Tamil astrological system organizing the 27 Nakshatras into five bird groups. Each person's birth bird cycles through five states of power throughout the day. By knowing your bird's current state, you can choose the most auspicious times for any activity.

### 3. Hora Shastra (होरा शास्त्र)
The science of planetary hours, dividing each day and night into 24 segments, each governed by one of the seven classical planets in the Chaldean order. Activities aligned with the ruling planet's nature are said to yield better results.
