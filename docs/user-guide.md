## What is Saranidhi?

**Saranidhi** (சரநிதி) means **"The Treasure House of Breath"** — a profound combination of two ancient Sanskrit/Tamil words:

- **Sara (சர)** — Refers to *Swara* or *Charam*, the sacred flow of breath through the nostrils. In *Siva Swarodaya* and traditional Tamil *Sara Kalai* sciences, Sara is the dynamic life-force energy (Prana) that shifts between the Solar channel (Pingala — right nostril) and Lunar channel (Ida — left nostril).

- **Nidhi (நிதி)** — Means *Treasure*, *Repository*, or *Wealth*. It represents an inexhaustible storehouse of divine value.

Saranidhi is a spiritual life-guidance app that helps you treat your daily breath patterns as a form of divine wealth. By observing and aligning your internal breath flow with external cosmic currents, you unlock a state of optimal living, presence, and vitality.

## The Aim

Saranidhi exists to bridge the ancient wisdom of breath science with modern daily life. Its aim is threefold:

### 1. Awareness
Help you notice the subtle but powerful rhythm of your breath — which nostril is dominant right now, how long your breath flows, and what that means for your current state of being.

### 2. Alignment
Guide you to synchronize your personal breath rhythm with the cosmic patterns described in Siva Swarodaya — the hours of planetary power (Horas), the five elemental cycles (Tattvas), and the biological rhythms of the five sacred birds (Panja Pakshi).

### 3. Transformation
Through consistent daily observation and alignment, cultivate a conscious way of living where every breath becomes an act of spiritual practice — not something you do, but something you *are*.

## Core Concepts

### The Three Flows

Your breath flows through one of three states at any given moment:

| Flow | Nostril | Energy | Nature |
|------|---------|--------|--------|
| **Solar (Surya Swara)** | Right (Pingala) | Active, warming, masculine | Best for physical tasks, courage, difficult conversations |
| **Lunar (Chandra Swara)** | Left (Ida) | Receptive, cooling, feminine | Best for creative work, learning, peaceful activities |
| **Sushumna** | Both equally | Balanced, transcendent | Rare and sacred — ideal for meditation and spiritual practice |

### The Five Yamas (Time Segments)

Each day, from sunrise to sunset, is divided into **5 equal time segments** called Yamas. Each Yama activates a different quality of energy, mapped through the Panja Pakshi system.

### The Five Sacred Birds (Panja Pakshi)

Based on your birth star (Nakshatra) and the current lunar phase, one of five birds is assigned as your guiding bird:

| Bird | Quality | When Ruling |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Vulture (Hawk)** | Power, authority | Take bold decisions |
| **Owl** | Wisdom, insight | Study, plan, strategize |
| **Crow** | Movement, activity | Travel, communicate, network |
| **Rooster (Cock)** | Nourishment, growth | Eat, heal, build |
| **Peacock** | Grace, display | Present, perform, celebrate |

Each bird cycles through five states daily: **Ruling → Eating → Walking → Sleeping → Dying**. When your bird is *Ruling*, you are at peak power. When *Dying*, conserve energy.

### Rahu Kaal (The Shadow Period)

Each day contains one inauspicious window called **Rahu Kaal** — a period governed by the shadow planet Rahu. During this time, Saranidhi signals caution: the Oracle Readiness drops to 10%, advising you to avoid starting new ventures.

### Hora (Planetary Hours)

Each hour of the day is governed by a planet in the Chaldean sequence. Knowing the active Hora helps you choose activities aligned with planetary strengths (e.g., Jupiter's hora for learning, Venus's hora for relationships).

### Tattva (Elemental Breath)

Within each breath cycle, the five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether) rotate in sequence. Advanced practitioners can sense which element is active by observing breath characteristics — length, warmth, and direction of airflow.

## The Science Behind Saranidhi

Saranidhi is built on three ancient Indian scientific systems:

### 1. Siva Swarodaya (शिव स्वरोदय)
A text of 395 sutras in which Lord Shiva reveals to Parvati the science of breath. It describes how the alternation of breath between nostrils governs health, fortune, and spiritual progress. The text provides specific guidelines for which activities to perform based on the active nostril.

### 2. Panja Pakshi Shastra (பஞ்ச பக்ஷி சாஸ்திரம்)
An ancient Tamil astrological system organizing the 27 Nakshatras into five bird groups. Each person's birth bird cycles through five states of power throughout the day. By knowing your bird's current state, you can choose the most auspicious times for any activity.

### 3. Hora Shastra (होरा शास्त्र)
The science of planetary hours, dividing each day and night into 24 segments, each governed by one of the seven classical planets in the Chaldean order. Activities aligned with the ruling planet's nature are said to yield better results.
