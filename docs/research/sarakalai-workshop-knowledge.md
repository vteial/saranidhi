# Saranidhi — Sara Kalai Workshop Knowledge Base

> Canonical digitized capture of the owner's **Sara Kalai (சர கலை / the art of the breath-flow)**
> workshop and class knowledge. Source corpus: the owner's handwritten notes plus recorded
> Tamil classes. This document is **bilingual by design** — Tamil source terms are recorded
> first, each with transliteration and an English gloss. It is captured **incrementally, topic
> by topic**, across many sessions, so it is meant to be resumed and appended to rather than
> written in one pass.
>
> Scope is **Sara Kalai only** — the science of **சரம் (Charam / Swara / breath-flow)**.
> **பஞ்ச பட்சி (Panja Pakshi / five-birds) is intentionally out of scope here**: it is a
> dedicated separate class and will get its own document later (e.g. `panja-pakshi-workshop-knowledge.md`).
>
> This is the **source corpus from which backlog items are later derived** — but **capture ≠ backlog**.
> Each captured practice is classified by *how much the app can actually help* (see Legends). Some
> practices become strong app features; others are pure inner practice the app cannot deliver and are
> kept here as knowledge and context only.

---

## How to use this document

- **One topic per session.** Pick a Sara Kalai topic from your notes/recordings, capture it, then stop. Resume later using the Topic Progress Tracker.
- **Per practice, fill the template.** For every distinct practice inside a topic, copy the [Per-Practice Template](#per-practice-template) and fill each field.
- **Tag its app-help bucket.** Classify every practice 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 (see Legends). This is the most important judgement call in the whole document.
- **Mark whether it is already implemented.** We have shipped ~20 basic features; flag ✅ against any practice one of them already realizes, so this doc doubles as a coverage map.
- **Add a rough app idea only if it is obvious.** Leave it blank otherwise. Do not force app-ideas during capture — real backlog items are derived **later (Phase 2b)** from this corpus.
- **Keep Tamil terms with transliteration.** Always record the Tamil term first, then transliteration + English gloss, e.g. **சரம் (Charam / breath-flow)**.

### 1. Swara Fundamentals & Weekday Dawn Inception (அடிப்படை சர தத்துவமும் கிழமை தொடக்க விதிகளும் / Adippadai Sara Thathuvamum Kizamai Thodakka Vithigalum)

#### Practice — கிழமை & திதி சர தொடக்க விதி (Kizamai & Thithi Sara Thodakka Vithi / Weekday & Lunar Phase Dawn Swara Inception Rule)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Ensures health, prevents disease, and avoids financial loss or mental suffering (நோய், பொருள் இழப்பு, மனக்கஷ்டம் நீங்குதல்); guarantees the day begins auspiciously.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** At dawn, the breath must initiate in the prescribed nostril according to the weekday and lunar phase:
  - இடகைல / சந்திரகைல (Idakalai / Chandra Kalai / Left nostril): திங்கள் (Monday), புதன் (Wednesday), வளர்பிறை வியாழன் (Waxing Thursday / Shukla Paksha Thursday), வெள்ளி (Friday).
  - பிங்கலை / சூரியகைல (Pingalai / Surya Kalai / Right nostril): ஞாயிறு (Sunday), செவ்வாய் (Tuesday), தேய்பிறை வியாழன் (Waning Thursday / Krishna Paksha Thursday), சனி (Saturday).
- **App-help bucket:** 🟢 — The device can calculate the target swara from sunrise time, weekday, and lunar phase (tithi/paksha), and display the expected flow.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Daily dashboard card showing today's required dawn swara and target nostril.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, pp. 2–3

> ⚠️ **Pending Confirmation [CONF-001]:** Check whether Thursday Paksha split (வளர்பிறை vs தேய்பிறை வியாழன்) is calculated strictly by astronomical Tithi or sunrise civil day in recorded workshop video/handwritten notes.

#### Practice — காலை கண்விழித்தவுடன் சரம் பார்த்தல் (Kaalai Kanvizhithavudan Charam Paarthal / Checking Active Breath-Flow Immediately Upon Waking)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Immediate diagnostic of daily alignment; if the active nostril matches the day's rule, the day is confirmed auspicious; if opposed, alerts the practitioner to switch it before stepping out of bed to prevent illness.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Immediately upon opening the eyes in bed (ideally at Brahma Muhurtham 4:30 AM or within 20 minutes before/after sunrise), observe which nostril is flowing actively (பூரணம் / Puranam) and which is dormant (சூனியம் / Shunyam) without getting up. Once the day's first breath is verified and aligned, subsequent natural shifts during the day are harmless.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟢 — The device can send an awakening notification at sunrise/Brahma Muhurtham prompting a quick check-in to log the active nostril.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Morning one-tap check-in ("Which nostril is active right now?") with immediate feedback on whether it matches the day's rule.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, pp. 2–4

#### Practice — பிரம்ம முகூர்த்த / அமுதகாற்று விழிப்பு (Brahma Muhurtha / Amutha Kaatru Vizhippu / Brahma Muhurtham & "Elixir Breeze" Awakening)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** According to Vallalar (இராமலிங்க வள்ளலார்), waking in this two-hour pre-dawn window bestows the entire day's spiritual energy and yields a thousand-fold return on inward focus; fosters internal purity (அகத்தூய்மை).
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Awaken during பிரம்ம முகூர்த்தம் (Brahma Muhurtham / 1.5 to 2 hours before sunrise, around 4:00 AM – 6:00 AM / 4:30 AM). Without needing to leave the bed, observe the breath flow, turn to the required side if swara correction is needed, and engage in breath awareness, prayer, inward repetition of seed syllables/mantras (e.g. அங், மோ, ஓம் / Ang, Moh, Om), or inner stillness.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — The app can dynamically compute Brahma Muhurtham from astronomical coordinates and provide a gentle wake-up chime or a scaffolded 10-minute in-bed meditation timer, but cannot perform the inner contemplation.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Dynamic Brahma Muhurtham alarm with a guided audio chime for in-bed breath observation.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, pp. 4, 9–10

#### Practice — விடுபட்ட சரம் சரிசெய்தல் (Vidupatta Charam Sari Seythal / Catch-Up Rule for Missed Dawn Swara)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Fallback alignment when a practitioner forgets to check their breath at dawn; ensures adverse planetary/weekday effects are neutralized for the rest of the day.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** If morning breath observation was forgotten upon waking, whatever breath is flowing at the exact moment the practitioner first remembers (whether morning, noon, or evening) is treated as the day's baseline. If it opposes the day's prescribed swara, immediately switch it using somatic methods. Once aligned to the correct side, subsequent automatic switching is normal and requires no further intervention.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟢 — App can detect when the user opens the app for the first time in the day, prompt a catch-up check, and calculate whether an immediate shift is needed.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** "First check of the day" catch-up dialog triggered on initial app launch if the morning check was missed.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, pp. 9–10

#### Practice — சுழுமுனை தியானம் (Suzhumunai Dhyanam / Sushumna Dual-Flow Contemplation)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Transcending mundane activities; when both nostrils flow together, external worldly works (காரியங்கள்) will not succeed, but it is the supreme window for meditation and spiritual attainment.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** When breath is observed flowing equally through both nostrils (சுழுமுனை / Sushumna), abstain from starting any physical, commercial, or worldly actions. Sit quietly in dedicated meditation or continuous breath awareness (விபாசனா / Vipassana style).
- **App-help bucket:** 🔴 — Pure inner meditative absorption / cessation of external action; the app cannot generate this inner state and acts only as doctrinal knowledge / warning against outer work.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):**
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, pp. 2, 4

### 2. Somatic Methods for Swara Switching (சரத்தை மாற்றும் உடலியல் முறைகள் / Sarathai Maattrum Udaliyal Muraigal)

#### Practice — எதிர் பக்கவாட்டு படுக்கை முறை (Ethir Pakkavaattu Padukkai Murai / Lateral Recumbency on the Opposite Side)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Effortless somatic swara switching while remaining in bed upon waking; avoids forceful breathing or artificial manipulation.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Lie on the opposite side of the nostril you wish to activate:
  - To activate வலதுகைல / சூரியகைல (Right nostril / Pingala): Turn and lie on the left side, fold the left arm comfortably under the head, keep legs extended, rest the right hand on the right thigh, and observe the breath until it automatically shifts to the upper (right) nostril.
  - To activate இடகைல / சந்திரகைல (Left nostril / Ida): Reverse the position by lying on the right side.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — The app cannot lie down for the user, but can scaffold the practice with an illustrated posture card, step-by-step guidance, and a 3–5 minute countdown timer with a completion chime.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** "In-bed Swara Switch" card with posture illustration and a 3-minute breath-settling timer.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 3

#### Practice — யோக தண்டம் / அக்குள் அழுத்தம் (Yoga Dandam / Akkul Azhuttham / Axillary Crutch or Armpit Pressure Method)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** The classical Siddha/Muni technique for rapid, reliable swara switching without needing to lie down.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Place a folded heavy cloth/towel (கனமான துண்டு) or a traditional yogic staff (யோக தண்டம் / Yoga Dandam) firmly into the armpit of the side you wish to close. Firm pressure under the left armpit opens the right nostril (Pingala); firm pressure under the right armpit opens the left nostril (Ida).
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — The app can provide an illustrated somatic guide on how to apply armpit pressure using common household props (folded towel) along with an interval timer.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Somatic prop intervention guide with timer.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 3

#### Practice — கால் மேல் கால் முறை / யோக ஆசனம் (Kaal Mel Kaal Murai / Seated Cross-Legged Thigh-Pressure Posture)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Switching swara while sitting upright without needing props or lying down.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Sit upright and cross one leg over the opposite thigh (as famously depicted in Shirdi Sai Baba portraits):
  - Crossing the right leg over the left thigh directs breath flow into the right nostril (சூரியகைல / Pingala).
  - Crossing the left leg over the right thigh directs breath flow into the left nostril (சந்திரகைல / Ida).
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — Scaffoldable via visual posture cue and posture duration tracking.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** "Desk / Seated Swara Switch" posture guide.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 3

#### Practice — கை ஊன்றி சாயும் முறை (Kai Oondri Saayum Murai / Seated Single-Hand Floor Pressure with Lateral Lean)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** A subtle, discreet somatic intervention that can be done while sitting on a chair or floor without attracting attention.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** While seated, press the palm of the hand on the side you wish to close firmly against the floor or chair seat, leaning the torso slightly into that hand. Pressing the left hand with a slight left lean opens the right swara; pressing the right hand opens the left swara.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — Scaffoldable via a discreet micro-intervention prompt card and gentle timer.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** "Discreet Swara Shift" micro-card for quick reference during meetings or travel.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 3

#### Practice — காலை ஊன்றி எழும் முறை (Kaalai Oondri Ezhum Murai / First-Step Floor Pressure Upon Rising)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Somatic grounding and immediate alignment at the transition from sleep to waking activity.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** When stepping out of bed onto the floor, firmly press down the foot corresponding to the side where you want the breath to flow. Firm initial floor contact with that foot encourages the breath to shift to that side.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — The app cannot step for the user, but can scaffold this as a morning wake-up checklist prompt.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Morning routine checklist prompt: "Step onto the floor with your prescribed active foot."
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 3

> ⚠️ **Pending Confirmation [CONF-002]:** `saravithai.pdf` states pressing a foot activates that same side's nostril (direct). Cross-verify with recorded video/oral notes whether this pressure acts directly or contralaterally.

#### Practice — பஞ்சு அடைத்தல் முறை (Panju Adaithal Murai / Nostril Occlusion with Clean Cotton)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Enforcing prolonged, continuous flow through one nostril (e.g. for full 12-hour solstitial alignment) when physical postures cannot be held for extended periods.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** Gently insert a clean, loose plug of medical-grade cotton wool (சுத்தமான பஞ்சு) into the nostril that needs to be suppressed (e.g. gently plugging the left nostril to maintain unbroken flow in the right nostril from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM).
- **App-help bucket:** 🟡 — Scaffoldable with preparation instructions, safety guidance (hygiene, not packing too deeply), and periodic comfort reminders.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Solstitial alignment protocol guide with safety checklist.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 14

### 3. Solstitial Swara Alignment (அயன சரம் / Ayana Charam)

#### Practice — அயன சரம் முதல் நாள் விரத ஆயத்தம் (Ayana Charam Mudhal Naal Viratha Aayattham / Eve of Solstice Purification Fasting)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Purifies the physical and nadic channels (பரிசுத்தமாக இருத்தல்) so the breath flows unhindered during the sensitive solstitial transition.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** On the eve of the solstice (the day before தை 1 or ஆடி 1), consume only a single daytime meal (பகலில் ஒருபொழுது மட்டுமே உணவு உண்டு) and maintain purity of body, speech, and mind.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟢 — The device can compute the solar ingress date, alert the practitioner 24 hours in advance, and deliver fasting/preparation guidelines.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Annual Solstice Eve push notification with a preparation checklist.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 7

#### Practice — உத்தராயண சரம் — தை 1 விடியல் 5 நாழிகை இடகைல நிறுத்துதல் (Uttarayana Charam — Thai 1 Vidiyal 5 Nazhigai Idakalai Nirutthuthal / Uttarayanam Dawn 5-Nazhigai Left Swara Alignment)
- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Automatic annual recalibration; aligns the practitioner with the Sun's northward course (உத்தராயணம்) so that daily swaras throughout the year align naturally without constant manual correction.
- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):** On Thai 1 (தை முதல் தேதி), 5 நாழிகைகள் (5 Nazhigais = 2 hours = 120 minutes) before sunrise, observe and maintain unbroken breath flow strictly in the left nostril (இடகைல / சந்திரகைல) for the full 5-nazhigai duration, overriding the standard weekday rule.
- **App-help bucket:** 🟢 — Device can calculate astronomical sunrise, compute the exact 5-nazhigai pre-dawn window, sound an early alarm, and run a 120-minute alignment countdown.
- **Implemented?:** ⬜
- **Rough app idea (optional):** Uttarayanam Special Alignment Mode with pre-dawn audio wake-up and timer.
- **Source ref:** `saravithai.pdf`, p. 7

- **How it's practiced (brief mechanics):**

If they stand on your empty/dormant side (சூனியம் / Shunyam), it is false and will not materialize.

If breath is flowing on that side (left nostril), the first-mentioned contender (e.g. India) wins; if flowing in the opposite nostril (right), the second-mentioned contender (e.g. Australia) wins.

- **Purpose / benefit (the why):** Determining the active swara of another person (or animal/bird) without inspecting their nostrils, enabling diagnosis of their intentions and energetic state.

  4. **திசை & உயர இருப்பு (Spatial Position):** Querent standing in front, left, or higher ground = Left swara (இடகைல); standing behind, right, or lower ground = Right swara (சூரியகைல). *(Fallback if ambiguous: use the standard weekday dawn planetary swara).*
