# Saranidhi — Sara Kalai Workshop Knowledge Base

> Canonical digitized capture of the owner's **Sara Kalai (சர கலை / the art of the breath-flow)**
> workshop and class knowledge. Source corpus: the 3 foundational classical texts (`saravithai.pdf`,
> `sugam_tharum_swasa_kalai.pdf`, `gana_sara_nool.pdf`) plus the complete authentic 13-day 2025 Tamil video
> workshop corpus (Days 01 to 13). This document is **bilingual by design** — Tamil source terms are recorded
> first, each with transliteration and an English gloss. It is captured **incrementally, topic
> by topic**, across many sessions, so it is meant to be resumed and appended to rather than
> written in one pass.
>
> Scope is **Sara Kalai only** — the science of **சரம் (Charam / Swara / breath-flow)**.
> **பஞ்ச பட்சி (Panja Pakshi / five-birds) is intentionally out of scope here**: it is a
> dedicated separate class and will get its own document later (e.g. `panja-pakshi-workshop-knowledge.md`).
>
> This is the **source corpus from which backlog items are later derived** — but **capture ≠ backlog**.
> Each captured practice is classified by *how much the app can actually help* (see Legends). Some
> practices become strong app features; others are pure inner practice the app cannot deliver and are
> kept here as knowledge and context only.

---

## How to use this document

- **One topic per session.** Pick a Sara Kalai topic from your notes/recordings, capture it, then stop. Resume later using the Topic Progress Tracker.
- **Per practice, fill the template.** For every distinct practice inside a topic, copy the [Per-Practice Template](#per-practice-template) and fill each field.
- **Tag its app-help bucket.** Classify every practice 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 (see Legends). This is the most important judgement call in the whole document.
- **Mark whether it is already implemented.** We have shipped ~20 basic features; flag ✅ against any practice one of them already realizes, so this doc doubles as a coverage map.
- **Add a rough app idea only if it is obvious.** Leave it blank otherwise. Do not force app-ideas during capture — real backlog items are derived **later (Phase 2b)** from this corpus.
- **Keep Tamil terms with transliteration.** Always record the Tamil term first, then transliteration + English gloss, e.g. **சரம் (Charam / breath-flow)**.
