## English Conceptual Synthesis & Lineage Invariants

### 1. The Master Law of Somatic Swara Switching
- Across all physical techniques, intervention is **strictly applied to the Poornam (Active) side to force flow into the Shoonyam (Empty/Dormant) side** (`பூரணப் பக்கத்தில் பயிற்சி; சூன்யத்தில் பயிற்சி இல்லை`).
- Mechanical compression, pressure, or downward weight on the active side increases vascular and neuromuscular resistance on that flank, causing the hypothalamic autonomic centre to reflexively decongest the contralateral nasal erectile tissue, opening the opposite swara.

### 2. The 3-Step Mana Guru / Pineal Cognitive Invariant
- Physical technique alone is mechanical; authentic Sara Kalai requires neuromuscular-endocrinological intent focused at the **Mana Guru (மன குரு / Pineal Gland / Ajna Center)**:
  1. **தகவல் (Information):** Consciously observe and mentally register the current active swara and the intended target swara.
  2. **அனுமதி (Permission):** Request the autonomic master gland (Pineal) to permit and execute the shift.
  3. **நன்றி (Gratitude):** Express mental gratitude once the transition completes.

### 3. Absolute Sushumna Switching Prohibition (சுழிமுனை எச்சரிக்கை)
- **Strict Lineage Invariant:** When breath is flowing simultaneously through both nostrils (Sushumna / நடுநாசி), **no swara switching exercise may ever be performed**. Dual flow represents the sacred portal of cosmic communion and inner dissolution; attempting mechanical inversion during Sushumna is strictly forbidden and disrupts the prana.

### 4. Comprehensive Inventory of Somatic Switching Modalities
1. **Axillary Spherical Object (Ball Method):** Tennis ball or firm sphere pressed into Poornam armpit.
2. **Folded Towel (Thundu Method):** Folded towel tucked into Poornam armpit (historic origin of shoulder towel and panchayat court waist-tying rules).
3. **Seated Chair with Kolusu Varmam & Trunk Twist:** Cross Shoonyam leg over Poornam leg, grasp Kolusu Varmam (malleolus) with Shoonyam hand, twist torso towards Poornam side.
4. **Cross-Legged Floor Padmasana with Heel-to-Ischium:** Poornam heel pressed into opposite buttock, Shoonyam leg crossed on top, Kolusu Varmam grip, torso twisted towards Poornam side.
5. **4-Point Lateral Recumbency (Ear Seal & Kolusu Varmam):** Lie on Poornam side, seal lower ear with lower palm, bend upper leg and grasp upper Kolusu Varmam with upper hand. Lineage's most potent and rapid technique (2–5 mins).
6. **Standing Wall-Support, Big-Toe Grounding & Pendulum Leg (New Practice 73):** Poornam palm placed high above head level on wall, Poornam big toe pressed firmly into floor, Shoonyam leg lifted 1 inch and kept limp/dangling like a loose pendulum.
7. **Asymmetric Weighted Walking / Bucket Method (New Practice 74):** Carry weighted load (bucket with water) in Poornam hand and walk at a medium pace. Dynamic locomotion forces rapid contralateral shift.
