## Key Corroborations & Insights for Knowledge Base

1. **Hierarchy of Siddha Shastras:**  
   Gopalakrishnan ranks classical Siddha predictive sciences:
   - #1: Sara Shastram (சர சாஸ்திரம் — Science of Breath)
   - #2: Panja Pakshi Shastram (பஞ்சபட்சி சாஸ்திரம் — Science of Five Birds)
   This directly confirms the doctrinal tradition observed across both workshops.
2. **Tantric 5-Feather Talisman (பஞ்சபட்சி இறகு ரகசிய தாயத்து):**  
   - Source: Classical palm-leaf manuscript secret (*பஞ்சபட்சி சாஸ்திர ஏடு*).
   - Ingredients: Shed down/feathers (*பொங்கு*) of all 5 birds: Cock/Rooster (சேவல்), Owl (ஆந்தை), Crow (காகம்), Peacock (மயில்), Eagle/Vulture (கழுகு/வல்லூறு).
   - Encasement: Pure Silver Amulet (*வெள்ளி தாயத்து*).
   - Consecration (உருவேற்றுதல்): 21 consecutive days, 1,008 japa daily using the exact Maha Panchakshara: **"ஓம் நமசிவய"** (strictly terminal short vowel 'ya', not long 'yaa').
3. **Overcoming 'Padubakshi' (படுபட்சி தோஷ நிவர்த்தி):**  
   Solves a major practical challenge in Panja Pakshi: when an individual's ruling bird is afflicted or enters *Marana* (death) / *Padubakshi* days/yamas, this five-element talisman harmonizes all five elements simultaneously, preventing catastrophic failures and planetary doshas.
