## Key Corroborations & Insights for Knowledge Base

1. **Independent Doctrinal Identity vs Sara Kalai:**  
   Healer Baskar explicitly demarks the two classical disciplines: Sara Kalai is somatic and breath-flow dependent (நாசி சுவாசம்), whereas Panja Pakshi is macrocosmic, stellar, and birth-time/element dependent. They are mutually autonomous systems, but synergistic when practiced together ("எக்ஸ்ட்ரா பூஸ்டர்").
2. **Birth Bird Determination Dual-Track:**  
   Corroborates Mrs. Sathya's Day 1 curriculum:
   - Primary: Date of birth / Janana Nakshatra determines a single, invariant birth bird.
   - Secondary / Alternative: For individuals without verified birth time/horoscope, the first letter of their common name determines their bird, bifurcated into Valarpirai and Theipirai rulership.
3. **Strategic Application Domains:**  
   Direct validation of the 5 core practical use-cases:
