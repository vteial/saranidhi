## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Translation
"In Travel Swara (Payana Sara Kalai), when we travel to a destination but make an intermediate stop along the way, how do we calculate the governing swara? Furthermore, we select a swara suitable for travel direction, but upon arriving, the specific work to be performed requires an opposite swara—how do we reconcile this conflict?

Understand with precision: **Travel Swara applies ONLY to the exact instant of departure (*payanam ārambikkira andha vinādiyil*)!**

We are at location A and heading to location B. We determine the cardinal direction of the destination, ensure the corresponding swara is flowing, take our initial steps, and depart. That is all!
Five, ten, or sixty minutes into the trip, the breath will naturally alternate between nostrils. You must realize: Travel Swara only governs the moment of departure. If your journey takes 3 or 8 hours, it is impossible and physiologically unnatural for that same nostril to flow unbroken the entire time. The travel inception is sealed the moment you step out. After 2 minutes of walking, completely forget about the travel swara!

Do not constantly test your breath throughout the journey. Once you reach the destination, check your swara solely for the specific task you are about to execute.

**Resolving Multi-Stop Journeys:**
If undertaking a journey with multiple stops (e.g., traveling to propose marriage, but stopping for minor chores on the way), use one of three hierarchical protocols:
1. **Ultimate Destination Rule (Primary):** Calibrate your departure swara according to the cardinal direction of the final, overarching destination.
2. **Cardinal Priority Rule:** If executing 5 errands, calibrate departure swara according to the single most critical and consequential task of the day.
3. **Stage-by-Stage Inception Rule:** Calibrate swara for the first destination; upon concluding business there, re-check and recalibrate swara for departure to the next destination.

Do not allow Sara Kalai to become an obsessive psychological burden (*tension āyidum*). Apply it mindfully at critical departure thresholds, take your auspicious step, and proceed with peace of mind."

### 2. Core Operational Rules
- **Instant of Inception Seal:** Swara is verified strictly at the threshold of departure; post-departure physiological alternation is completely disregarded.
- **Directional Alignment Matrix:**
  - North & West = Chandra Nadi (Left Nostril / Ida).
  - South & East = Surya Nadi (Right Nostril / Pingala).
