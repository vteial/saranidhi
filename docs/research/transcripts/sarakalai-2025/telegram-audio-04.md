### 1. English Translation
"Regarding Vidiyal Sara Kalai Sadhana: Many participants have doubts on this, so I am providing this audio with complete clarity. Listen attentively.

There is a fundamental doctrinal difference between **Vidiyal Sara Kalai (விடியல் சரகலை / Awakening Breath Practice)** and **Udhaya Sara Kalai (உதய சரகலை / Solar Dawn Inception Practice)**:

- **What is Udhaya Sara Kalai?** It is the practice strictly anchored to the exact mathematical second of astronomical sunrise (*sūriyan udhikkum andha vinādiyilirundhu*), where on each day of the week, a specific nostril must run for a prescribed duration (1 or 2 hours). That is a distinct astronomical discipline!
- **What is Vidiyal Sara Kalai?** It is completely different. One person wakes up at 4:30 AM; another at 8:30 AM; another at 11:30 AM. Someone wakes 1 hour before sunrise; another wakes 3 hours after sunrise. Whenever you awaken from your night's sleep and prepare to step out of bed to begin your day, you perform this practice! Understand this first:
If you wake up at 4:30 AM, do Vidiyal Sara Kalai, you must NOT go back to sleep. It is performed when you are finally getting up to begin your active day. **It has ZERO relationship to solar sunrise! It has ZERO relationship to weekday (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.)!**

Do not look at the clock; do not consult the calendar/weekday. Whenever you wake up, perform this sadhana and start your day. Nor is it an obligatory burden—if you have time and inclination, do it; otherwise, just wake up and carry on your routine.

**Handling Immediate Bodily Urges:**
If upon waking you urgently need to urinate, or pass bowel motions, or drink water—by all means, relieve yourself first! There are no rigid dogmas here. Drink water, use the restroom, and then return to perform the sadhana.
If you had to open your eyes to switch off an alarm, that is completely fine. Do not be paralyzed by rigid fanaticism. Simply close your eyes and resume.

**The Step-by-Step Procedure:**
1. Sit in Sukhasana (cross-legged) upon waking. Eyes closed. Do not look at directions or clocks.
2. Conduct Nadi Pariksha (breath test) to identify Poornam (active) and Shoonyam (dormant).
3. Perform **Guhai Dhyanam (Cave Meditation)** 3 to 9 times (inhale active, natural retention, exhale dormant). '3 to 9' is simply a general guideline—you can do 1 repetition or 50, according to your available time.
4. Once completed, **adopt the Prayer Posture (பிரார்த்தனை நிலை):**
   - Prop up the knee of the **Shoonyam leg** (குத்த வைத்து / foot flat on the bed, knee upright).
   - Place the palm of the **Poornam hand** firmly on the bed/floor beside you.
   - Maintain this posture for 1 to 2 minutes (maximum 3 minutes).
   - Offer your morning prayers/affirmations here. (Kumbhaka is only for internal pranayama; do NOT hold your breath for 3 minutes during this prayer!).
5. Stand up on your bed or floor. Open your eyes.
6. **First Step Law:** Step forward with the **Poornam foot (active nostril foot) first!**

**The Ultimate Reconciliation (CONF-013 Resolution):**
Only if you happen to awaken within the first 1 to 2 hours of astronomical sunrise should you align with **Udhaya Sara Kalai** (consulting weekday/tithi dawn rules to revive or maintain the designated solar/lunar channel). At all other waking times, you practice **Vidiyal Sara Kalai**, completely free of weekday or solar dawn constraints!"

### 2. Resolution of CONF-013
- **Doctrinal Hierarchy Established:**
  - **Udhaya Sara Kalai (உதய சரகலை):** Classical Astronomical Dawn Rule. Strictly anchored to astronomical sunrise (06:00 AM civil/astronomical horizon). Governed by Weekday/Tithi cycles (e.g., Sunday Surya 1 hr, Monday Chandra 1 hr, Tuesday Surya 2 hrs, etc.).
  - **Vidiyal Sara Kalai (விடியல் சரகலை):** Somatic Bedside Awakening Sadhana. Completely independent of solar sunrise and weekday schedules. Governed exclusively by the moment of somatic awakening from sleep.
  - **Synthesis:** The two systems never conflict because they operate in distinct contexts. If awakening coincides with astronomical dawn (first 1–2 hours of sunrise), Udhaya Sara Kalai takes precedence. At all other waking hours, Vidiyal Sara Kalai applies.
