## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Translation
"You stated that one should bathe when the Right Swara (Surya Nadi) is active. But bathing inherently cools the body down, right? So when the Right Swara is running, the body actually needs heat—this creates confusion.

What you must understand is: in classical Sara Kalai texts, they have laid down that bathing should occur under the Right Swara. That is all. It does not mean you must dogmatically bathe *only* when the Right Swara is running.

I have explained the Core Thermoregulation Principle (37°C Internal Homeostasis). If you comprehend this principle, you will find the definitive answer to this question:
When the Right Swara is running, the internal system requires heat. Therefore, you may bathe in lukewarm/warm water. Whether it should be mildly warm or hotter depends upon the geographic climate and ambient temperature.

For instance, if someone is in a freezing sub-zero region (-10°C) where snow is falling, and they check their breath to find the Right Nostril active, they can bathe in hot water to preserve core heat.
If someone in Tamil Nadu has the Right Nostril active, they can take a mildly warm water bath.
However, if the **Left Nostril (Chandra Nadi)** is active, one should **never bathe in hot water**, because the Left Swara is already actively reducing heat to cool the body down!

Therefore, when bathing, consider which country/climate you inhabit, whether it is day or night, which swara is presently running, and adjust water temperature (cold, lukewarm, or hot) based on your physiological thermoregulatory needs.

Once this is grasped, the rigid question of 'which nostril must I bathe under' dissolves. The ancients documented the general rule; I reiterated it. But understanding the core temperature principle resolves not just bathing, but all related somatic queries. This is why I repeatedly emphasize thermoregulation."

### 2. Doctrinal Rules & Water Matrix
- **Classical Rule:** Bathing is ideally conducted during Right Swara (Surya Nadi / Pingala) to maintain digestive fire (*jataragni*) and prevent dampness/Kapha stagnation.
- **Thermoregulation Synthesis (Healer Baskar Lineage Rule):**
  - **Surya Nadi Active (Right Nostril):** Body indicates an internal need for warmth. Use lukewarm or hot water (calibrated to geographic climate).
  - **Chandra Nadi Active (Left Nostril):** Body indicates excess internal heat and is actively cooling. Do **NOT** use hot water, as artificial external heating clashes with internal cooling mechanisms. Use room-temperature/normal water.
