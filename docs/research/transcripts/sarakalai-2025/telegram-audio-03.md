## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Translation
"You taught that inhaling through the dormant nostril (Shoonyam / சூன்யம்) is strictly forbidden as a cardinal law of Sara Kalai. Yet during Saturn Transit (Shani Peyarchi / ஏழரை நாட்டு சனி) and Ayana Sara Kalai (solstitial alignment), you instruct practitioners to deliberately inhale only through Shoonyam and plug the active nostril (Poornam / பூரணம்) with cotton! This creates apparent doctrinal conflict: why enforce Shoonyam inhalation here if it is forbidden?

Understand the somatic reality of Shoonyam inhalation. First, examine what harm occurs when you inhale through Shoonyam:
Suppose at 4:00 PM your body is alternating nostrils to restore internal balance and regulate temperature by 5:00 PM. If you artificially force inhalation through the dormant Shoonyam nostril, your physiology is thrown into reverse gear (*reverse-la poga ārambichiruvōm*).
It is like driving from Chennai to Kanyakumari, reaching Melmaruvathur, suddenly making a U-turn back to Tambaram, and then resuming the journey. Forcing Shoonyam inhalation reverses physiological momentum, producing acute side effects (imbalance, headaches, energetic depletion). That is why the classical masters declared it strictly forbidden.

HOWEVER, the planetary impact of Ezharai Sani (7.5-year Saturn transit) or intense astrological ingresses is astronomically massive compared to temporary somatic discomfort.
Consider this financial analogy:
Inhaling through Shoonyam incurs a somatic loss of ₹100.
Undergoing an adverse Saturn transit in an unshielded heating Pingala swara incurs a devastating loss of multi-crores!
Comparing a ₹100 minor loss against a multi-crore catastrophe, any wise person willingly absorbs the ₹100 loss to avert the catastrophic multi-crore ruin!

During Ayana Sara Kalai and Shani Peyarchi, enforcing Shoonyam inhalation creates minor transient discomfort (perhaps 3 days of mild somatic sluggishness). But allowing Pingala (Surya Swara) to run during Saturn's malefic ingress locks the individual into severe afflictions for **2.5 to 7.5 uninterrupted years**!
Between 3 days of minor somatic recalibration and 2.5–7.5 years of planetary suffering, the defensive seal of Shoonyam is overwhelmingly superior. This is the exact rationale for this exception."

### 2. Critical Doctrinal Principles
- **The Absolute Rule:** Shoonyam Inhalation is forbidden during standard daily sadhana, eating, travel, bathing, and normal pranayama.
- **The Astrological Ingress Defense Exception (Saturn / Solstice Protocol):**
  - When a major planetary or celestial transit demands pure lunar cooling (Chandra Swara / Left Nostril), and the practitioner finds the Right Nostril active (Poornam), the practitioner **MUST plug the Right Nostril with cotton** and breathe exclusively through the Left Nostril (even though it was dormant Shoonyam).
  - **Risk-Reward Tradeoff:** 3 days of mild doshic imbalance versus 2.5–7.5 years of catastrophic karmic/health affliction (*Ezharai Sani*).
