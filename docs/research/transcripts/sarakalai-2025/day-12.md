## English Conceptual Synthesis & Lineage Invariants

### 1. Sushumna Duration Ceiling & Transition Invariant (சுழிமுனை கால வரம்பு)
- **4-to-5 Minute Hard Ceiling:** Sushumna dual-nostril flow never persists continuously for more than **4 to 5 minutes**, even in supreme Jnanis/Siddhas.
- **Universal Transition Bridge:** Airflow never jumps directly from Left to Right or Right to Left without passing through Sushumna as the neutralizing transitional bridge.
- **Cellular Self-Repair Sanctuary:** Worldly activity is barred during Sushumna because the autonomic nervous system utilizes this brief window for cellular repair, brain clarification, and cosmic pranic recharging.

### 2. The 4-Day Sustained Nostril Pathology Threshold (மரண & மனநோய் எச்சரிக்கை)
- **Continuous Unbroken Left Swara (≥4 Days):** Indicates imminent physical departure / death (Marana Lakshana).
- **Continuous Unbroken Right Swara (≥4 Days):** Indicates severe neurological overheating and oncoming psychiatric breakdown / mania (*பைத்தியம் பிடித்தல்*).

### 3. Contralateral Whole-Body Biofield Circuit (எதிர்-முனை பிராண ஓட்டம்)
- Inhaled breath entering the **Right Nostril (Surya)** circuits through the body down to the **Left Big Toe**.
- Inhaled breath entering the **Left Nostril (Chandra)** circuits down to the **Right Big Toe**.
- This fully corroborates the foundational Contralateral Pressure Invariant (CONF-002).

### 4. Psychological & Behavioural Swara Diagnostics (மனமும் சரமும்)
- **Truth vs. Evasion Invariant:**
  - **Left Swara Active:** Natural inclination toward moral integrity, truthfulness (*உண்மை பேசுதல்*), and calm conviction.
  - **Right Swara Active:** Natural tendency toward emotional reactivity, tactical deception, evasion, or exaggeration (*பொய் பேசுதல்*).
- **Speech Cadence Diagnostic:**
  - Dragged-out, sing-song cadence (*இழுத்து ராகத்தோடு பேசுவது*): Right Swara.
  - Crisp, sharp, authoritative cadence (*நறுக்கென்று கட் அண்ட் ரைட்டாக பேசுவது*): Left Swara.
- **Equanimity Pacing Sadhana (நிதான சரகலை):** When scattered or agitated, face East and execute gentle, slow, single-nostril inhalation and exhalation through the active Poornam channel.
