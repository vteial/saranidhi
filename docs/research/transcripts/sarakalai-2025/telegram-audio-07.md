## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Translation
"Which swara should be active when ascending hills or mountains?

Understand the somatic physiology: ascending staircases or climbing mountains demands vigorous physical exertion that elevates core heat. Therefore, **you must climb while the Right Nostril (Surya Nadi / Pingala) is active!**

You continue climbing under Surya Nadi. If you suddenly experience acute exhaustion and fatigue, test your breath: invariably, your system will have switched to the **Left Nostril (Chandra Nadi)**! The internal metabolic heat spiked excessively, so the autonomic system engaged Chandra Nadi to cool down, inducing exhaustion.

Do not halt the instant Chandra Nadi opens unless you feel genuine fatigue. The practical protocol is:
- Begin ascending under **Right Swara (Surya)**.
- Continue ascending until you feel fatigued.
- Check your breath:
  - If the Right Nostril is still active despite feeling slightly tired, you retain reserve stamina and can climb further.
  - If the Left Nostril has taken over and you feel genuinely exhausted, **sit and rest immediately!**
- You may resume climbing as soon as you feel refreshed, or for optimal traditional precision, rest until the breath naturally or through posture reverts back to the **Right Nostril (Surya)**, then resume climbing.
- When fatigue recurs, sit, rest until Surya returns, and climb again.

However, do not fall into dogmatic neurosis. The fundamental somatic reality is simple: when fatigued, sit and rest; when energized, climb. You do not need complex intellectual gymnastics for common-sense physiology. Only consult the breath when in doubt whether to push through or rest."

### 2. Operational Rules for Climbing & Physical Ascent
- **Ascent Initiation:** Always step and ascend under **Surya Nadi (Right Nostril / Pingala)**, which provides cardiovascular vigor and skeletal strength.
- **Fatigue Diagnostic:** The spontaneous switch to **Chandra Nadi (Left Nostril)** during heavy ascent signifies that core metabolic heat has reached capacity and cooling rest is physiologically mandatory.
- **Recovery Rule:** Rest until Surya Nadi re-activates before initiating the next phase of ascent.
