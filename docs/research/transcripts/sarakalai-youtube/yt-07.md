## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Summary
Master S. Gopalakrishnan presents an authentic therapeutic application of breath inversion for self-healing:
1. **The Four Classical Tamil Shastras (நாலும் தெரிந்தவன்):**
   - 1. *Sara Shastram* (The science of respiratory currents & prana)
   - 2. *Panja Pakshi Shastram* (The fivefold biorhythmic cycle)
   - 3. *Vaidya Shastram* (Siddha medicine and pharmacology)
   - 4. *Jyothida Shastram* (Astrology and time calculation)
2. **Nadi Polarity:**
   - Right Nostril: *Pingala / Surya Kalai* (Solar / Heating).
   - Left Nostril: *Ida / Chandra Kalai* (Lunar / Cooling).
   - Both Nostrils: *Sushumna / Sulimunai* (Central Axis).
   - Active flow: *Charam / Poornam*. Inactive flow: *Shoonyam*.
3. **The 21-Rep Therapeutic Inversion Protocol:**
   - Applied whenever bodily disease or pain manifests (fever, migraine, chronic/acute illness).
   - Timing: Dawn around 6:00 AM (*விடியற்காலை 6 மணி*).
   - Diagnostic: Check which nostril flows dominantly.
   - Action: **Inhale strictly through the active (flowing) nostril, and exhale strictly through the dormant (inactive / Shoonyam) nostril!**
   - Execution Count: **Exactly 21 repetitions** once daily at dawn.
   - Treatment Duration: Practice for 5 consecutive days until symptoms fully resolve, then cease.
