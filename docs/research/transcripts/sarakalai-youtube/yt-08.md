## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Summary
Master S. Gopalakrishnan reveals an accessible contemplative practice designed to dissolve mental distress, chronic vacillation, and subconscious fear:
1. **Morning Awakening Positioning:** Immediately upon getting up from bed in the morning, seat oneself facing East (*கிழக்கு நோக்கி அமர்தல்*) with an erect spinal axis (*முதுகுத்தண்டு நேராக*).
2. **Unilateral Focus on Pingala (Surya Nadi):** Close eyes and maintain passive, unforced awareness strictly on the airflow entering and exiting the **Right Nostril** for **15 minutes**. Even though breath naturally flows through both nostrils, conscious attention is anchored exclusively to the right channel.
3. **Passive Respiration Law:** Do not execute forceful pranayama or deliberate inhalation/exhalation; simply observe natural somatic airflow.
4. **Psychological & Energetic Outcomes:**
   - Eradicates mental confusion and induces profound cognitive clarity (*மனத்தெளிவு*).
   - Eliminates chronic grief and vacillation (*சஞ்சலம் நீங்குதல்*).
   - Dissolves irrational, subconscious fear (*தேவையற்ற பயம் விலகுதல்*).
   - Neutralizes stress and restores vitality.
