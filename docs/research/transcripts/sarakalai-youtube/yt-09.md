## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Summary
Master S. Gopalakrishnan provides the full foundational theory and practical execution methodology for pranic longevity:
1. **The Biological Breath Budget:** Human lifespan is allocated as a fixed reservoir of breaths (metaphor of the 14.5 kg LPG gas cylinder). Consuming the allocation rapidly through rapid, agitated breathing precipitates premature death, whereas decelerating the respiratory rhythm proportionally extends chronological life toward the natural 120-year potential.
2. **The 21,600 Daily Breath Ledger:** Corroborates the classic Siddha baseline of 21,600 breaths every 24 hours. Physical and psychological dissipation triggers (running, oversleeping, excessive ejaculation, anger, grief, malice) drastically accelerate breath turnover beyond 50,000/day, draining vitality.
3. **The Breath-Space-Light Progression (*வளி $\rightarrow$ வெளி $\rightarrow$ ஒளி*):**
   - Mastering *Vali* (respiratory control) unlocks *Veli* (akashic / spatial ether awareness).
   - Mastering *Veli* merges the individual consciousness into *Oli* (transcendent divine inner light / Soruba Samadhi).
4. **Pranayama Warm-up & Alternate-Nostril Respiration:**
   - Detailed preparatory joint mobilization (neck, eyes, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles).
   - Alternate nostril breathing (Nadi Shuddhi / Anuloma Viloma) in Vishnu Mudra.
   - Decelerating a single cycle to 1 to 3 minutes conserves vital prana.
5. **Strict Safety Cap & 48-Day Mandala Progression:**
   - **Crucial Caution:** Beginners must NEVER over-practice. Straining causes intense visceral heat (*உஷ்ணம்*), ophthalmic inflammation, and cutaneous eruptions.
   - **Mandala Schedule:** Exactly **5 minutes morning & evening for the first 48 days (1 mandala)**. Increment by only 5 minutes per 48-day cycle ($5\text{m} \rightarrow 10\text{m} \rightarrow 15\text{m}$).
