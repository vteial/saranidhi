### 1. English Summary
In this 10-minute video, Healer Baskar shares an alternative chronobiological bedtime formula titled the "Deep Sleep Opposite Swara Formula":
1. **The Inverse Inception Axiom:** To achieve restorative nocturnal sleep (*ஆழ்ந்த தூக்கம்*), one must enter sleep when the active nostril is the **EXACT CONTRALATERAL OPPOSITE of tomorrow morning's required dawn inception nostril**!
2. **Weekly Nighttime Sleep Schedule Derived from Dawn Inception:**

   - **Monday Night:** Tuesday dawn requires Right $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Left Swara (Chandra)**.
   - **Tuesday Night:** Wednesday dawn requires Left $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Right Swara (Surya)**.
   - **Wednesday Night (Krishna Paksha):** Thursday dawn requires Right $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Left Swara (Chandra)**.
   - **Wednesday Night (Shukla Paksha):** Thursday dawn requires Left $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Right Swara (Surya)**.
   - **Thursday Night:** Friday dawn requires Left $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Right Swara (Surya)**.
   - **Friday Night:** Saturday dawn requires Right $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Left Swara (Chandra)**.
   - **Saturday Night:** Sunday dawn requires Right $\rightarrow$ enter sleep in **Left Swara (Chandra)**.
