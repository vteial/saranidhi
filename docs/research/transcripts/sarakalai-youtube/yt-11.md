## English Translation & Doctrinal Gloss

### 1. English Summary
Master S. Gopalakrishnan provides a ritualized morning contemplative sadhana targeting mental perplexity, emotional irritability, and cognitive dullness:
1. **Ritual Inception Day:** Must be initiated on a **Sunday (ஞாயிற்றுக்கிழமை / Surya Day)** at dawn.
2. **Sadhana Altar & Environment:**
   - Evacuate bowels and bladder; perform ablutions of face, hands, and feet.
   - Spread a **pure white mat or cloth (*வெள்ளை விரிப்பு*)** on the floor.
   - Light an **earthen oil lamp (*அகல் விளக்கு*)** beside the seat.
   - Seat oneself **facing East (*கிழக்கு நோக்கி அமர்தல்*)** in Sukhasana or Padmasana, maintaining an erect spinal column and level head.
3. **Passive Pingala Observation:**
   - Close eyes.
   - Rest complete attention exclusively on the right nostril aperture (*வலது மூக்கு ஓட்டை*).
   - Observe the natural, unforced inward and outward flow of breath through the right channel for **exactly 15 minutes**.
   - Zero voluntary forcing or forceful pranayama.
4. **Therapeutic Outcomes:**
   - Dissolves deep-seated mental confusion (*மனக்குழப்பம்*).
   - Eliminates chronic agitation and anxiety (*சஞ்சலம்*).
   - Removes feelings of helplessness / depression (*இயலாமை*).
   - Pacifies chronic irritability, short temper, and annoyance (*எரிச்சல் குணம்*).
   - Sharpens neuro-cognitive faculties and memory (*புத்தி கூர்மை, மூளை தெளிவு*).
