Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2, 2026

Saranidhi ("the App") is a spiritual breath-tracking application that respects your privacy absolutely. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Data We Collect

None. Saranidhi does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on developer-owned servers. All data you enter remains entirely on your device.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your display name (optional, entered during onboarding)
  • Your birth star (nakshatra) and derived Panja Pakshi bird
  • Your location coordinates (for sunrise/sunset calculation only)
  • Breath journal entries (timestamps, nostril flow, alignment, timer durations)
  • App preferences (theme, language, notification settings)

This data is stored in a local SQLite database and never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

3. Optional Cloud Backup

If you choose to enable cloud backup, your data is encrypted and stored in your own cloud account:

  • iOS: Your personal iCloud account (via Apple Sign-In)
  • Android/Web: Your personal Google Drive App Data folder (via Google Sign-In)

The developer has no access to your cloud backup data. It resides solely in your personal Apple or Google account.

4. Analytics & Tracking

None. Saranidhi does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels. There are no third-party services that receive your data.

5. Network Usage

The App operates fully offline. All Vedic calculations (sunrise/sunset, Panja Pakshi, Yama, Rahu Kaal, Hora, Tattva) are performed locally using pure mathematical algorithms. No network connection is required for core functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that access your personal data. The only external connections are:

  • Apple/Google authentication (only if you enable cloud backup)
  • iCloud/Google Drive storage (only if you enable cloud backup)

7. Children's Privacy

Saranidhi does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App does not collect data from anyone — all information stays on your device.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • In-app: Settings → Clear All Data (permanently removes all local data)
  • Cloud backup: Managed through your Apple/Google account settings
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all local data

9. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and within the App. Material changes will be communicated through the App's update notes.
